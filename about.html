Hi, I’m Brendan, an engineering manager with way too many hobbies and not nearly enough hours in the day. By trade I work in tech, but my interests sprawl pretty far beyond the keyboard: gaming, anime, AI development, card games, board games, tennis, pickleball, and a violin that deserves more practice than it gets.

I live with my wife Lei, and together we travel whenever we can and eat spicy food at every opportunity. We also share our home with two cats, Yuna and Leo, who spend a suspicious amount of time doing absolutely nothing productive.

Most of my spare hours lately go into small apps and games: Kinma, Capsule Cove, and CatDex. They’re where I get to play with AI, pixel art, and whatever else I’m curious about that month. This site is a place to collect some of what I make and where I go.
